C100 virtual keyboard

Try the 4 layers and their pages before flashing. It runs the same logic as firmware v13: 4 layers and 19 pages, latch and sustain, scales (Western and Non-Western), arpeggiator, rolled chords, effects, sequencers, Euclidean, CC sequencer, harmony, microtonal and generative pages. Touch screens can hold several keys at once.

Layer 1 Page 1 Controls Shift Latch Arp Board rec Ableton clock Scale C Major Quantize Octave 0 Channel 1 120 BPM

Pages: PG▲ / PG▼ (R1C9, R2C9) step through pages 1-4 of a layer. L▲ / L▼ (column 10) change the layer. Controls Shift (R1C3): hold it and the control keys change job (for example R2C4 = Ctrl+U, R1C5 = Ableton record). Hold + arrows: hold Scale, Tempo, Octave, Root, Channel or Volume (or a keyboard or polyrhythm setting key) and use the arrows: up / down small step, right / left big step. Scale: right / left switches Western / Non-Western. SUS (R10C10): hold = sustain, double-tap = latch on / off. ARP (R10C9): double-tap = arpeggiator on / off, hold = arp settings on the top 4 rows. Effects (layer 3 page 4): tap an effect to edit its faders, double-tap to switch it on / off. Play / stop (R1C4) starts the internal clock for the arpeggiator and sequencers, or turn on the Ableton clock below.

Sound and MIDI

Real MIDI output works in Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone. It is not available in Safari on iPhone.
