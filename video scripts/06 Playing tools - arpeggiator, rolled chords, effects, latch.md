## Narration only

Hold three notes. Listen.

That is the arpeggiator. Double-tap the A R P key at the bottom of the side columns to switch it on, and again to switch it off.

Hold the key and the pads turn into settings: rate, mode, octaves and gate, and a rhythm mask. Tap one to change it.

Hold ARP and tap up: patterns, shown with two letters. A L is Alberti, B A is Bach, R O rolling, B E bebop, U S upper structure, C S cadence stop, I N intensity.

The keys have no velocity, they are on or off switches. So the Intensity pattern gets denser and louder the longer you hold the chord.

Rolled chords are for the chord pages. Normally a chord starts all at once. Tap the Roll key, R8C9, and the notes come one after another, like a harp or a guitar.

Hold the Roll key and use the arrows: up and down change the speed, right the direction, left the velocity ramp.

The effects key gives you MIDI effects for everything you play: echoes, chorus, ratchets and more. Tap it to arm effects. Hold it to see them, and double-tap an effect to switch it on. Now every note comes back as echoes.

F X off switches them all off again.

Last: sustain. Hold the key for a sustain pedal. Double-tap it for latch: play a chord, let go, and it keeps playing until you tap the pads again.

These four tools work on almost every page. Next, layer two: controlling Ableton itself.
