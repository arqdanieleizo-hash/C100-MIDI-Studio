## Narration only

This is a keyboard with a hundred keys. Watch what it does when I change one setting.

In this series I will show you how I turned a Keychron C100 into a MIDI controller for Ableton Live.

The C100 is a giant macro pad: a hundred keys in a ten by ten grid. With new firmware, every key sends MIDI instead of typing letters.

The bottom left, eight by eight, is the pad grid. This is where you play. It changes completely from page to page.

The two rows on top are control keys. They stay the same everywhere: play, record, tempo, scale, and the most important one: Shift.

The columns on the right are side keys that each page uses for its own settings. And the four keys in the top-right corner are arrows. Hold Shift and they become navigation.

There are four layers. Layer one: keyboards. Layer two: Ableton control. Layer three: sequencers. Layer four: generative music. Each layer has several pages.

From drum pads and chord keyboards, to a clip launcher, an XY effects pad, sequencers, and a pond that plays music from ripples.

You do not have to guess. There is a virtual keyboard to test everything in your browser, a planner to design your own layout, and a guide that explains every page.

In the next episode we will put the firmware on the keyboard and connect it to Ableton.

If you want to follow along, the guide and the virtual keyboard are free to open in any browser. See you in the next one.
