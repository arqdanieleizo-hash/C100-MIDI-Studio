## Narration only

Three keyboards for when the first four are not enough.

Harmony is a keyboard for writing. The left half holds the chords of the key. The right half is a melody keyboard, with only notes of the scale.

Hold a chord, and the melody side lights up to tell you which notes fit: chord tones, good tensions, and notes to avoid.

The four keys on the right are a chord progression. Tap a step, then tap a chord. Do it for all four.

Press Play, and the four chords loop. Now play a melody over your own progression.

Multi channel gives you four small keyboards, one octave each, every one on its own MIDI channel.

Hold a keyboard's channel key and tap the arrows to change its channel. Octave and volume work the same way.

The bank keys give you four banks, sixteen keyboards in all, on all sixteen channels.

Microtonal gives you notes between the notes. Six tunings: twenty-four, nineteen, and thirty-one tones per octave, just intonation, the harmonic series, and Bohlen-Pierce.

Each note is sent with its own pitch bend, so you need an instrument that understands M P E. In Ableton, switch on MPE for the track input.

Seven ways to play. Next: tools that make any of them more interesting: the arpeggiator, rolled chords, effects and latch.
