## Narration only

Layer one is where you play notes. Seven pages, four of them in this episode.

Page one, the Composer: drums top left, a sixteen step sequencer top right, and two pianos below. Each has its own MIDI channel.

To make a beat, tap a drum pad, then tap steps on the right. The sixteen steps read like text, left to right, top to bottom.

Press Play, and the white light runs over the steps. That is your loop.

The same sequencer can record a melody. Hold the target key, tap a piano key, then tap a step and press the notes.

Page two, Chromatic. Every pad is a note. One pad to the right is one semitone higher, one row up is eight higher. A chord shape you learn once works in every key.

The lights show your scale: roots are bright, other scale notes a second colour, and notes outside the scale are dark.

Page three, In key. Every pad is in the scale. You cannot play a wrong note. Right is the next note of the scale, up is three scale steps.

Change the scale and the same pads play something else, still in key.

Page four, Chords. One pad, one chord. The columns are the chords of the key. The rows are chord types: triads at the bottom, then inversions, sevenths, ninths, suspended chords, and power chords on top.

Four rows up, seventh chords. Same degree, richer sound.

Four ways to play notes. In the next episode: the harmony keyboard, many channels at once, and microtonal tunings.
