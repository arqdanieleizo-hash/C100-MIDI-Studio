## Narration only

Four layers, nineteen pages, and one key to move between them all.

This is Shift. It is the third key on the top row. Hold it, and the control area turns orange. Shift has one job only: moving between layers and pages.

While you hold Shift, tap the up arrow: next layer. The column of lights on the right shows where you are: one light for each layer.

Tap the right arrow for the next page. The second column counts the pages. When you let go, a label such as P three flashes for a second.

Down and left go the other way. Without Shift, the arrows are just arrow keys.

Now the control keys. Many are settings with no value of their own. You hold the key and tap the arrows. Hold Scale, tap up: the next scale. The pads spell its name in two letters.

Hold Root: the home note moves up a semitone every tap. Left and right move by a fifth.

Tempo: up and down add one beat per minute, right and left add ten.

Octave: right goes up a whole octave for every keyboard. Channel sets the MIDI channel for the whole board. Volume works the same way.

The other control keys do one thing when you tap them: play and stop, board record, tap tempo, undo, quantize and pitch bend. The full list, with their Shift jobs, is in the guide. One more: the clock key on the side, R6C10, switches the clock between Auto and Internal. In Auto, the board follows Ableton's clock when one arrives.

Colours help: an orange key is switched on. A cyan key does something different on this page.

With Shift and the four arrows you can reach every page. Next, we start playing: layer one.
