## Narration only

Before you flash anything, test it here.

This is the same board, in your browser. Click or touch the keys. On a touch screen you can press several at once. The monitor shows every MIDI message, and the meters show every knob.

It can play a built-in sound, or send real MIDI to Ableton, so you can try a new layout without touching the keyboard.

The guide explains every page. Use the map at the top, or the menu on the left.

Each page has a labelled picture. The numbered circles match the list under the board.

Press Play to watch a demo. Step moves one idea at a time. Drag the slider to jump anywhere. And the box under the board shows exactly what the keyboard would send.

Open the list at the bottom of a card and you get the exact job of every key, taken from your layout file, so it is always up to date.

Dark or light, your choice.

The animations are not videos: they run the same program as the keyboard. Next episode: design your own layout.
