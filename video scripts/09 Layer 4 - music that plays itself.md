## Narration only

Layer four does not need you to play. You set the rules and it plays.

Random walk. A walker moves one step each beat. Where it stands decides the note: left to right is pitch, bottom to top is how hard.

You can have four walkers, each with its own colour and its own MIDI channel. The keys on the right select a walker, and switch it on or off.

Hold a setting key on the side and tap the arrows: speed, step size, how strongly the walker is pulled to the centre, and more.

Pond. The grid is a water surface. Tap a pad: a pebble drops, and ripples spread. Where ripples cross, their heights add up, and a cell that gets high enough plays its note.

Big waves are loud and bright, fading ripples are soft and muffled. Hold a setting key on the side and tap the arrows to change speed, decay, threshold, wavelength, how far a ripple reaches, and more.

Constellation. Tap pads to place stars. They are joined by a path, and a white playhead runs along it. On a star it plays a held chord, on the cells between it plays short notes.

Add a star, and the whole melody changes. The arrows change the tempo and how far stars can connect.

Three ways to let the board play. Next, a short look at the tools around it, starting with the virtual keyboard.
